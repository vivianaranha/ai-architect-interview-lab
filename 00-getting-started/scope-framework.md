# SCOPE Framework

## S — Situation and stakeholders
Who uses the system? What workflow or decision changes?

## C — Constraints and success criteria
Latency, cost, data sensitivity, availability, scale, compliance, and human approval.

## O — Options and architecture
Present viable alternatives, then explain your chosen design.

## P — Production concerns
Security, reliability, observability, governance, failures, versioning, cost.

## E — Evaluation and evolution
Offline evaluation, production metrics, feedback, regression tests, rollback, improvement.

# Whiteboard Challenge 15

## Prompt
Design a disaster recovery for mission-critical AI.

## Include
- Users and goals
- Functional/nonfunctional requirements
- Components and data flow
- Identity/trust boundaries
- AI/model/retrieval/tool choices
- Failure handling
- Evaluation and observability
- Cost and trade-offs

## Self-score
Rate 1–5: framing, architecture clarity, security, production readiness, trade-offs, communication.

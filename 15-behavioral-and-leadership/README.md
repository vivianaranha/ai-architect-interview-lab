# Behavioral and Leadership Questions

## 1. Tell me about a time you disagreed with an AI architecture.

**Guidance:** Show decision context, evidence, alternatives, stakeholder alignment, and outcome.

Use STAR/CAR, but emphasize architectural judgment and influence.

## 2. Tell me about a failed AI proof of concept.

**Guidance:** Own the result, identify wrong assumptions, explain learning and architectural changes.

Use STAR/CAR, but emphasize architectural judgment and influence.

## 3. How do you explain AI risk to executives?

**Guidance:** Translate technical risk into impact, likelihood, mitigations, ownership, and options.

Use STAR/CAR, but emphasize architectural judgment and influence.

## 4. What if a customer insists on an LLM when rules are better?

**Guidance:** Respectfully compare alternatives using reliability, cost, explainability, and measurable outcome.

Use STAR/CAR, but emphasize architectural judgment and influence.

## 5. How do you resolve build-vs-buy disagreement?

**Guidance:** Use differentiation, time, control, integration, skills, cost, risk, lock-in, and exit strategy.

Use STAR/CAR, but emphasize architectural judgment and influence.

## 6. How do you lead architecture without authority?

**Guidance:** Shared goals, evidence, design reviews, ADRs, prototypes, influence, and clear ownership.

Use STAR/CAR, but emphasize architectural judgment and influence.

## 7. How do you manage technical debt in fast AI delivery?

**Guidance:** Make it visible, classify risk, prioritize high-impact debt, and reserve capacity without blocking learning.

Use STAR/CAR, but emphasize architectural judgment and influence.

## 8. How do you communicate uncertainty?

**Guidance:** Separate facts from assumptions, use evaluation evidence, quantify where possible, and explain safeguards.

Use STAR/CAR, but emphasize architectural judgment and influence.

## 9. How do you mentor AI engineers?

**Guidance:** Teach framing, trade-offs, evaluation, security, operations, debugging, and communication—not only libraries.

Use STAR/CAR, but emphasize architectural judgment and influence.

## 10. What are your first 90 days as AI architect?

**Guidance:** Map stakeholders, systems, portfolio, standards, risks, reference architectures, delivery bottlenecks, and high-value wins.

Use STAR/CAR, but emphasize architectural judgment and influence.

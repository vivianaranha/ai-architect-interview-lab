# Mock Interview 3: AI Platform Architect

## Format
- 10 min discovery
- 30 min architecture
- 10 min trade-offs
- 10 min leadership

## Focus
- Model gateway
- Multi-tenancy
- Quotas
- Governance
- Developer experience

## Required follow-ups
1. What ships in 4–6 weeks?
2. Biggest production risk?
3. Human approval points?
4. How do you evaluate quality?
5. How do you reduce cost?
6. How do you handle provider change?
7. What do you log?
8. What would you not build?

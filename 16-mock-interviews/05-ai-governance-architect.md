# Mock Interview 5: AI Governance Architect

## Format
- 10 min discovery
- 30 min architecture
- 10 min trade-offs
- 10 min leadership

## Focus
- Inventory
- Risk tiering
- Evidence
- Approval workflows
- System cards

## Required follow-ups
1. What ships in 4–6 weeks?
2. Biggest production risk?
3. Human approval points?
4. How do you evaluate quality?
5. How do you reduce cost?
6. How do you handle provider change?
7. What do you log?
8. What would you not build?

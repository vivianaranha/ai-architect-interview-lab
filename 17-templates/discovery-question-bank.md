# Discovery Question Bank

## Business
- What problem are we solving?
- Who experiences it?
- What is the current workflow?
- Which business metric should improve?
- What is the cost of an incorrect answer/action?

## Data
- What are the systems of record?
- How fresh must data be?
- What is sensitive?
- Which permissions must be preserved?

## Scale
- Users, request volume, peaks, latency, availability?

## Governance
- Regulations, audit needs, human approval, retention?

## Operations
- Owner, SLOs, on-call, model outage behavior, rollback?

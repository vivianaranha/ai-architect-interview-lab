# Interviewer Guide

## Suggested format
1. 5–10 min discovery
2. 25–35 min design
3. 10 min deep dive
4. 5–10 min trade-offs
5. 5–10 min leadership

## Strong signals
Clarifies ambiguity; connects design to outcomes; separates deterministic/probabilistic logic; understands identity and permissions; discusses failures, evaluation, cost, and rollout; explains trade-offs clearly.

## Weak signals
Immediately names tools; overuses agents; ignores auth; no evaluation; no operations; no cost awareness; treats prompts as security controls.
